Trasper is a staff-only quotation and pro forma system for travel agencies. Build branded options
from a pasted PNR or from scratch, add named passengers or traveller groups, issue an itinerary and
a pro forma invoice, then record and reconcile payments. Itineraries can also be created standalone —
by hand or from pasted text — when no quotation or pricing is needed.

## The short version

1. Add the paying customer and link the travellers, or quote a traveller group before the names are known.
2. Create a quotation, then build one or more options. Enter the flights and costs by hand, or paste
   a PNR when a booking already exists.
3. Mark the quotation sent. It locks, freezes its totals and becomes the customer document.
4. Accept an option and issue the itinerary and pro forma.
5. Record payments. Pro forma status is derived from the ledger, never typed.

## Scope and limits

- **Pasted text only.** Trasper does not call a GDS or airline API; the converter reads text you
  paste. Flights can also be entered by hand when there is no text to read.
- **Pro formas, not final invoices.** The document flow stops at a pro forma and its payment ledger;
  final invoices and receipts are outside this build.
- **No customer mailbox.** Staff record calls, emails and messages in the timeline. Resend is used
  only for staff 2FA, recovery, password reset and invites.
- **No passport data.** Collect and check travel documents outside the system.
- **Word is internal.** The Word export is for staff who can see costs; PDFs are the customer
  deliverable.
- **Single-tenant SQLite.** The app needs a persistent disk and is not designed for a serverless
  filesystem.

<Callout type="info" title="Agency-neutral by design">
  Company name, address, logo, footer, bank details and time zone live in Settings. Nothing about a
  particular agency is compiled into the source code.
</Callout>
