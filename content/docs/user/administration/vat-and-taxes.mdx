---
title: VAT and taxes
description: VAT rate, registration, rounding unit and reusable optional tax presets.
---

**Settings → VAT and taxes** defines the agency's tax rules. VAT is calculated on the service fee
(markup) only, never on the supplier cost and never on other taxes.

## VAT

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| VAT rate (%) | Entered as a percentage (for example `16.5`); stored as basis points (`1650`). |
| VAT registration number | Printed when tax rows are shown and on explicit-VAT pro formas. |
| VAT/GST label | Customer-facing name, for example VAT, GST or Sales tax. |
| Rounding unit (minor units) | Totals are rounded once at the end. `1` = smallest unit, `100` = nearest whole currency unit. |

VAT is enabled or disabled per quotation: inherit, force on or force off. The agency switch is the
default.

## Optional tax presets

Presets are reusable across quotations. A single option may enable several at once.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Printed on itemised tax layouts. |
| Kind | `fixed` (amount) or `percent` (basis points). |
| Value | Minor units for fixed, basis points for percent. |
| Enabled | Disabled presets are ignored by the calculation. |
| Percentage base | `cost`, `markup` (service fee) or `subtotal` (cost + markup). |

Examples: hotel service tax on cost, airport fee as a fixed amount, city tax on the subtotal.

## Calculation order

1. Convert each cost line at the option's stored rate.
2. Sum the converted costs.
3. Calculate markup (percentage of cost, or lump sum).
4. Calculate VAT on the markup only.
5. Add enabled optional taxes on their configured base.
6. Total = cost + markup + VAT + optional taxes.
7. Round once to the rounding unit.
8. Allocate the rounded total back over the lines proportional to cost; the last line absorbs the
   remainder.

VAT is never charged on cost or on another tax. See [pricing rules](/docs/user/reference/pricing) for the
full invariants and worked examples.

## Presentation

Tax rows appear only according to the [tax layout](/docs/user/administration/pricing-presentation). The
calculation base is independent of whether the customer sees a tax row. Internal views always show
cost, markup, VAT, taxes, total and profit.

## Permission and audit

`manageSettings` is required. Every change is audited; presets are stored in `TaxPreset` and copied
into an option's `TaxLine` rows when used.
