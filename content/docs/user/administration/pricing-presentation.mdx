---
title: Pricing presentation
description: Choose how customers see prices, service fees and taxes without changing the calculation.
---

**Settings → Pricing presentation** controls the default customer-facing layout. Each quotation can
override the price and tax layouts.

<Callout type="info" title="Presentation only">
  Layouts never change the calculation. Cost is converted, markup and VAT are calculated, the total
  is rounded once, and the rows are a view of that result. Every layout must sum to the same total.
</Callout>

## Price layouts

| Layout | Customer sees | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lump_sum` | One all-inclusive amount per flight or service. | Traditional quotations. |
| `net_plus_fee` | Supplier price per line plus one labelled service fee. | Agencies that quote a transparent fee. |
| `itemised` | Supplier cost, service fee and each tax as separate rows. | Tax-invoice-style documents. |
| `per_person` | All-inclusive lines plus a per-traveller price. | Packages and group trips. |
| `total_only` | A single package total with no line prices. | Tours and cruises that hide components. |

The service fee is derived as the difference between the displayed rows and the total. It is not a
separate calculation and cannot drift from the total.

## Tax layouts

| Layout | Customer sees |
| --- | --- |
| `included` | No tax row; line prices contain tax. VAT stays inside the lump sum. |
| `note` | Included, with a sentence confirming it. |
| `summary` | Line prices exclude tax; one combined taxes row before the total. |
| `itemised` | VAT and every enabled optional tax listed by name, rate and base. |

VAT is **always** calculated on markup only, regardless of the tax layout. The itemised tax layout
does not move VAT onto cost.

If you choose the itemised **price** layout but taxes are marked `included` or `note`, the tax layout
is promoted to `summary` so the service-fee row cannot silently absorb the tax.

## Editable labels

| Label | Default | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service fee | Service fee | `net_plus_fee`, `itemised` |
| Combined tax | Taxes | `summary` |
| VAT/GST | VAT | `itemised` tax rows |
| Total | Total | All layouts |
| Per-person | Price per person | `per_person` |
| Tax note | Empty | `note` and `itemised` |

Labels are customer-facing, so use the agency's own terminology. Keep them short enough for the
document table.

## Overrides

A quotation can choose its own price and tax layouts. `null` means "follow the agency default" and
is resolved when the document is built. The override is stored on the quotation and frozen into the
snapshot when sent.

## Accommodation lines

Unit-priced services show the quantity and unit, and an optional per-unit amount. The calculation
still uses the total cost; the unit amount is display metadata. See
[quotation editor](/docs/user/guide/quotation-editor#fares-and-services).
