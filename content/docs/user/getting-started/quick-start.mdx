---
title: Quick start
description: Sign in, complete the agency profile and issue a first quotation.
---

This page assumes the app is installed and seeded. If it is not, start with
[installation](/docs/user/getting-started/installation).

## 1. Sign in

Open the app at `http://localhost:3000`, choose **Staff sign in**, and use the administrator
credentials from `.env`. The public landing page is `/`; after sign-in the staff dashboard opens at
`/dashboard`. On first sign-in:

- Change the password under **Settings → Profile**.
- If the account or role requires two-factor authentication, you are redirected to
  `/enrol-2fa` before any other staff page opens. See
  [two-factor authentication](/docs/user/administration/security-2fa).

## 2. Complete the agency profile

Open **Settings → Company** and fill in:

- company name, address, email, phone, website and TIN;
- the time zone used for document dates, deadlines and reports;
- the document header: picture letterhead or typed company details with the logo;
- footer and internal notes.

Then set the commercial defaults on the same page: base currency, default margin, quotation
validity, exchange-rate warning age and ticketing warning window.

## 3. Configure tax and presentation

- **Settings → VAT and taxes**: turn VAT on if registered, enter the rate in percent, the
  registration number and the rounding unit in minor units.
- **Settings → Pricing presentation**: choose the default price layout (lump sum, price plus service
  fee, itemised, per person or single total) and the default tax layout.
- **Settings → Bank accounts**: add the account(s) printed on quotations and pro formas.

## 4. Add a customer and travellers

Create a customer under **Customers → New customer**, then add travellers under **Passengers** and
link them to the customer. See [customers](/docs/user/guide/customers) and
[passengers](/docs/user/guide/passengers).

## 5. Create the quotation

1. Open **Quotations → New quotation**.
2. Choose the customer, branch, currency, issue date and validity date.
3. Leave **VAT** on **Use agency default** unless this quotation needs an override.
4. Create the quotation. The editor opens.

## 6. Build an option

In the editor:

- Add flights with **+ Add flight**, or paste a PNR into **Import a PNR or itinerary** and choose
  **Read / convert** to fill the segments and passenger names.
- Add a named passenger fare, or a traveller group such as **2 Adults** when the names are not known
  yet, plus any services (hotel, transfer, visa, insurance and so on).
- Confirm the exchange rate, margin and optional taxes.
- Watch the internal total, cost, markup, VAT and profit update as you type.

See the [quotation editor](/docs/user/guide/quotation-editor) for every field.

## 7. Send and accept

- **Send** validates the draft, freezes the totals and company snapshot, and locks the quotation.
- When the customer chooses, mark the quotation **accepted** with the chosen option.
- Issue the [itinerary](/docs/user/guide/itineraries) and [pro forma](/docs/user/guide/proformas) from the
  accepted option.

## 8. Record payments

Open the pro forma and record a receipt. The status is derived from the payment ledger: unpaid,
part-paid, paid or cancelled. Mistakes are corrected with a reversing entry, never by editing or
deleting a payment. See [payments](/docs/user/guide/payments).

<Callout type="info" title="Itineraries without a quotation">
  If no price is involved, create a **standalone itinerary** from **Itineraries → New standalone
  itinerary**. It has its own customer, passengers and frozen flight snapshot, but no quotation,
  prices or pro forma.
</Callout>
