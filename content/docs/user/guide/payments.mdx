title: Payments
description: Record receipts, reverse mistakes, read the ledger and reconcile aged debtors.

Payments are append-only. Trasper never edits or deletes a payment; corrections are new reversing
entries linked to the original.

## Record a payment

Open a pro forma and choose **Record payment**.

| Field | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Cannot be in the future (agency time zone). |
| Amount | Must be greater than zero and cannot take the total above the pro forma total. |
| Method | Cash, bank transfer, mobile money, cheque or card. |
| Reference | Optional bank or receipt reference. |

The action re-checks the balance inside the transaction, so two accountants recording at the same
time cannot overpay. Recording a payment updates the derived status and writes the audit entry in
the same transaction.

## Reverse a payment

Choose **Reverse** on the ledger row and give a reason. This creates a negative entry with:

- the same method;
- today's date in the agency time zone;
- a reference beginning `Reversal:`;
- a link (`reversesId`) to the original payment.

A payment can be reversed at most once, enforced by a unique index and a transaction check. A
reversing entry cannot itself be reversed. A cancelled pro forma cannot take payments or reversals.

## Ledger

The **Payments** page (roles with `viewPaymentLedger`) shows every receipt and reversal with totals
for the current view:

- received;
- reversed;
- net;
- number of entries.

Filters cover the date range and payment method, with free-text search over the table. The ledger is
append-only and is the source of truth for balances. Selected rows can be exported to CSV.

## Aged debtors

**Reports → Aged debtors** lists outstanding pro forma balances by age:

| Bucket | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Current | Not yet due. |
| 1–30 | Up to 30 days past due. |
| 31–60 | 31 to 60 days. |
| 61–90 | 61 to 90 days. |
| 90+ | More than 90 days. |

Age is calculated from the due date (or issue date when no due date) to the reporting date in UTC
calendar days. Cancelled pro formas and zero balances are excluded.

The report exports as CSV from `/api/reports/aged-debtors` for roles with `viewReports`.

## Reporting interaction

Payments feed the reports page:

- **Payments received** is net of reversals.
- **Outstanding** is the balance at period end.
- **Profit** comes from the frozen quotation totals, not from payments.
