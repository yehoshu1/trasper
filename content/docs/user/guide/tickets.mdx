title: Tickets

A ticket is issued by the airline or its ticketing system. Trasper does not issue airline tickets; it
records the issued electronic ticket, keeps its coupons and produces a printable itinerary receipt.

## What an airline ticket contains

A standard electronic ticket has:

- a **13-digit ticket number** (the last digit is a check digit);
- the passenger name as held by the airline;
- the issue date, form of payment, plating carrier, pseudo-city and IATA number;
- one or more **coupons**, each with flight, class, date, route, departure time, status, fare basis,
  not-valid-before/after dates and baggage allowance;
- an electronic ticket record held by the airline, which is what airport staff scan at check-in.

The paper or PDF itinerary receipt is only a summary of that airline record. It is not the ticket
itself.

## Import tickets

Open **Tickets → Import tickets**. You can paste either or both:

- the airline `TKT:` block with the passenger, issue details and coupon lines;
- the `*HTE` electronic ticket list with names and ticket numbers;
- the airline **booking confirmation** (the “E-Ticket & Itinerary Details” document).

The ticket text and the booking confirmation do different jobs:

- the ticket text supplies the ticket numbers, passenger names, issue date, form of payment and
  fare basis;
- the booking confirmation supplies the full itinerary — arrival times, terminals, aircraft, meal
  service, baggage allowance and the booking and check-in references.

A group booking normally has one itinerary for every passenger. Leave **Apply the first itinerary to
every passenger** ticked so the same flights and references are stored on each ticket. Clear it to
keep only the first passenger's itinerary.

When a passenger has two ticket stocks, for example a Kenya Airways ticket and an Air Tanzania
ticket, paste the booking confirmation: it carries both numbers and Trasper stores them on one
ticket record. The primary ticket is the stock that matches the first flight.

Choose **Read / convert** to preview the ticket numbers, passenger names and flight counts, then
save. Trasper only accepts 13-digit ticket numbers and never invents a number. Duplicate ticket
numbers already in the system are skipped.

Optionally link the imported tickets to a customer and quotation. If a quotation is chosen, its
customer is used automatically.

## Ticket record

The ticket page shows:

- passenger, both ticket numbers, issue date, form of payment and plating details;
- booking and check-in references;
- every coupon with its flight, route, dates, departure and arrival times, terminals, class,
  aircraft, status, fare basis, baggage and meal service;
- the original pasted ticket text for audit and reconciliation.

Tickets are immutable records. A void, refund or reissue is a new airline record; import the
replacement ticket rather than editing the old one.

## Preview and PDF

- On-screen preview from the ticket record.
- PDF at `/api/pdf/ticket/[id]`.
- **Print all** on the ticket list renders every matching ticket into one PDF, one page per
  passenger. Search first to print a single group or batch.
- The receipt prints the company letterhead, passenger information, both ticket numbers, booking
  and check-in references, the itinerary with layover rows, standard travel notes and the closing
  thank-you line. Longer connections over 24 hours are destination stays, not layovers.
- It is not a boarding pass and does not replace the airline's electronic ticket record.

## Permissions

`manageDocuments` can import tickets. `viewDocuments` can see and print them.
