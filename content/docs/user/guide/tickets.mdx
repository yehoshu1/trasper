title: Tickets
description: Import airline-issued electronic tickets, store coupons and print ticket receipts.

A ticket is issued by the airline or its ticketing system. Trasper does not issue airline tickets; it
records the issued electronic ticket, keeps its coupons and produces a printable itinerary receipt.

## What an airline ticket contains

A standard electronic ticket has:

- a **13-digit ticket number** (the last digit is a check digit);
- the passenger name as held by the airline;
- the issue date, form of payment, plating carrier, pseudo-city and IATA number;
- one or more **coupons**, each with flight, class, date, route, departure time, status, fare basis,
  not-valid-before/after dates and baggage allowance;
- an electronic ticket record held by the airline, which is what airport staff scan at check-in.

The paper or PDF itinerary receipt is only a summary of that airline record. It is not the ticket
itself.

## Import tickets

Open **Tickets → Import tickets** and paste either:

- the airline `TKT:` block with the passenger, issue details and coupon lines; or
- the `*HTE` electronic ticket list with names and ticket numbers; or
- both together.

Choose **Read / convert** to preview the ticket numbers and coupon counts, then save. Trasper only
accepts 13-digit ticket numbers and never invents a number. Duplicate ticket numbers already in the
system are skipped.

Optionally link the imported tickets to a customer and quotation. If a quotation is chosen, its
customer is used automatically.

## Ticket record

The ticket page shows:

- passenger, ticket number, issue date, form of payment and plating details;
- every coupon with its flight, route, date, status, fare basis, validity and baggage;
- the original pasted ticket text for audit and reconciliation.

Tickets are immutable records. A void, refund or reissue is a new airline record; import the
replacement ticket rather than editing the old one.

## Preview and PDF

- On-screen preview from the ticket record.
- PDF at `/api/pdf/ticket/[id]`.
- The receipt prints the ticket number, passenger, issue details and coupon table. It is not a
  boarding pass and does not replace the airline's electronic ticket record.

## Permissions

`manageDocuments` can import tickets. `viewDocuments` can see and print them.
