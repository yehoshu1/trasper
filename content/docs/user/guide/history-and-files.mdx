## Activity timeline

Every quotation and customer has a timeline of sales activity:

| Type | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Called | A phone call |
| Emailed | An email sent outside the app |
| WhatsApp | A messaging conversation |
| Note | A general internal note |
| Meeting | An in-person or online meeting |

Each entry stores the body, when it happened and an optional next follow-up date. The author and
timestamp are shown; entries are audited.

The **next follow-up** date feeds the dashboard priority score and the notifications page. A
follow-up due today or earlier raises a notification.

<Callout type="info" title="No customer mailbox">
  The in-app mail client is not part of this build. "Emailed" records that an email was sent
  elsewhere. Transactional system mail is limited to 2FA, recovery, password reset and staff invites.
</Callout>

## Attachments

Attachments can be added to quotations and pro formas:

- maximum 10 MB; allowed types are PDF, PNG/JPEG/WebP, text, CSV, EML, Word, Excel and ZIP, with the
  extension checked against the declared MIME type;
- files are stored under `UPLOAD_DIR`, outside `public/`;
- downloads go through `/api/attachments/[id]`, which requires `viewDocuments` **and** `viewCosts`;
- archiving hides an attachment but leaves the stored file on disk;
- attachment metadata and actions are audited.

Attachments may contain supplier costs, which is why viewers never receive them.

## Auditing

Every write creates an `AuditLog` row in the same transaction as the change. Staff with `viewAudit`
can open **Settings → Audit log**. Cost-bearing fields and secrets are redacted for roles without
the relevant capability.

See [audit log](/docs/user/administration/audit).
